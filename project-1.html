PROJECT 01 / CASE STUDY

Business Analytics Dashboard

POWER BI · EXCEL

Project overview

An end-to-end retail sales analysis project using the Vrinda Store dataset. The project explores customer purchasing behaviour, sales performance, order status, product categories and regional sales distribution through an interactive Excel dashboard.

Process & key work

  • Collected and inspected retail sales data.
  • Cleaned and organized customer, product and order information.
  • Created Pivot Tables for sales and order analysis.
  • Analysed monthly sales and order trends.
  • Compared male and female customer purchasing behaviour.
  • Evaluated order statuses and sales channels.
  • Identified top-performing states and product categories.
  • Designed an interactive Excel dashboard using charts and slicers

Skills & tools

Microsoft Excel Data Cleaning Pivot Tables Pivot Charts Slicers Data Analysis Business Intelligence Data Visualization

Key findings

  • Women contributed approximately 64% of total revenue.
  • Amazon was the leading sales channel, contributing approximately 35.51%.
  • Maharashtra generated the highest sales among the listed states.
  • March recorded the highest monthly revenue.
  • Sets were the highest-revenue product category.
  • Approximately 92.25% of orders were delivered.
